# Marketing plan

**Status:** Proposed 2026-09-26 for owner approval. **Owner:** founder. Builds on the [brand strategy](../design/BRAND.md#brand-strategy); search work is in [SEO](SEO.md). Nothing here is spent or published without the owner's approval.

Oxinov markets to **everyone, worldwide**. Products are global by default: English first, more languages over time, local currencies and payment methods, and pages that work on any phone and connection. We choose where to push harder by evidence (sign-ups, conversion, support capacity, payment coverage), not by a fixed home market.

## Goal for the first 6 months

Win the **first 25 paying organisations** for Oxinov Edu, **in at least 5 countries**, with at least 1,000 active learners and a sign-up-to-first-course rate above 40%. Company-brand work supports that goal; other products get marketing when they launch.

## Market sequencing (by evidence, not borders)

| Wave | Where | Why | Needs before launch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Global English | Everyone who can use English, marketed online worldwide | Largest reach, no translation cost | English site, USD pricing, card payments |
| 2 — Early local markets | Countries where wave 1 shows the most sign-ups, plus the headquarters region where we can offer in-person support | Faster sales with local support and payments | Local-language pages, local currency and payment method, local support hours |
| 3 — Scale languages | Next languages chosen from demand data (for example Hindi, Japanese, Spanish, Arabic, Portuguese) | Search and word of mouth grow in each language | Native copy, script fonts, right-to-left layout for Arabic, local legal review |

Review the waves each quarter with the metrics below.

## Offer and funnel

1. **Attract:** posts, videos, search, referrals, and demos point to the Edu product page with one call to action: **"Start a free class"**.
2. **Try:** an organisation creates a space in minutes; a free plan (limits set with pricing) with one course and a class code.
3. **Activate:** the first course published and five learners joined within 7 days; help by chat or video if not.
4. **Pay:** upgrade in the customer's currency with a local payment method where available (payment providers per ADR-008; NPR, INR, USD, and more as providers allow); annual discount.
5. **Grow:** referrals, case studies with permission, renewals, and expansion to more teachers.

**Pricing principle:** one global price list in USD, with purchasing-power-adjusted local prices where fair, so products stay affordable in every country.

## Measuring

| Metric | Target by month 6 | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Countries with signed-up organisations | 10 | Edu database |
| Paying organisations | 25 in 5 or more countries | Billing |
| Demos booked | 80 | Contact form, chat, calls |
| Active learners (30 days) | 1,000 | Edu database |
| Visitors from search | 3,000/month | Search Console |
| Cost per paying organisation | under US$100 | Ad spend and sales time |

Website analytics: the site promises "no cookies and no analytics" today. Recommended: cookie-free, privacy-first analytics (for example self-hosted Umami on the k3s node, no extra cost) plus Google Search Console, after updating the privacy notice. Cookie-free analytics also avoids consent banners in most jurisdictions. This needs the owner's decision because it changes a published promise.

## Rules we follow

- **Honest claims:** no fake reviews, invented numbers, or exam-pass guarantees; practice scores are labelled as practice (FR-EXAM-1204).
- **Consent and law:** photos, names, and quotes only with written consent (from a parent for minors); marketing messages only to people who opted in; follow each market's privacy and marketing law (for example GDPR in Europe, CAN-SPAM in the United States, and local rules elsewhere) before advertising there.
- **Privacy:** no tracking pixels until the privacy notice and consent handling are updated per market.
- **Inclusion:** examples, names, and images from many countries and cultures; accessible content (captions, alt text, readable contrast).
- **Brand:** follow [BRAND.md](../design/BRAND.md) for logo, colors, fonts, and voice; native speakers for every non-English language.
