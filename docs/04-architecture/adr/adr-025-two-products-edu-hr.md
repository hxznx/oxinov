# ADR-025: Two product modules now—Oxinov Edu and unified Oxinov HR

**Date:** 2026-09-28. **Status:** Accepted (owner decision: the current product modules are HR and Edu; merge and remove the standalone Jobs and LMS FRD names). Supersedes ADR-022 only where it made Jobs a separate product plane and amends ADR-010 accordingly.

1. **Oxinov HR owns both recruitment modes.** The verified HR-professional network, managed recruitment from CV to deployment, and the direct employer-to-candidate job board are modules of one `hr` product plane. They use `hr-web`, `hr-api`, and database `oxinov_hr`; public job discovery is under `hr.oxinov.com/jobs`. No `jobs-web`, `jobs-api`, `oxinov_jobs`, `jobs.oxinov.com`, separate entitlement, or separate release gate is created.
2. **Permanent IDs survive the merge.** FR-JOB-6001 to FR-JOB-6092 remain unchanged and retain their approval and traceability, but live in `HR-FRD.md` beside FR-HR-8001 to FR-HR-8143. Direct applications and professional-managed engagements remain separate authorization modules inside the same product boundary; candidates explicitly choose or consent to each path.
3. **Oxinov Edu is the product name everywhere customer-facing.** Its canonical requirements file is `EDU-FRD.md`. The existing technical slug `lms` remains for source paths, package names, OIDC audience, database, and migrations because renaming a live product's internal identifiers would add deployment and data risk without customer value. “LMS” remains a generic description of a customer workspace, not a second product module.
4. **No runtime or cost change.** Neither HR nor a separate Jobs service exists today. This consolidation removes planned workloads and therefore adds no AWS spend. Oxinov Edu production paths and behavior do not change.

Consequences: customers see a simpler two-product model; employers can use one HR product for direct hiring or engage a professional; HR must enforce module-level access so a direct application never becomes an agency candidate record without consent. The standalone Jobs charter and product record are retired after their requirements and release gates are incorporated into HR. Alternatives considered: keeping Jobs separate (duplicate employer, candidate, search, messaging, moderation, and certificate capabilities) and renaming the live `lms` technical slug (high migration risk with no customer-facing benefit).
