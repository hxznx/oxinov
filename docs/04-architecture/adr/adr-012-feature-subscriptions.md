# ADR-012: Feature-based subscriptions governed by oxinov.com

**Date:** 2026-09-24. **Status:** Proposed (research). Oxinov uses one plan ladder (Free, Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise) across all products, with **Oxinov One** as the cross-product bundle and product-specific plans only where a product has a distinct buyer. Marketplaces also earn transaction commission and promotion fees. The platform control plane owns the plan catalogue, subscriptions, entitlement keys, usage limits, metering, and billing; products check entitlement keys and remaining limits, never plan names. `oxinov.com/pricing` and `app.oxinov.com/billing` are the governing places to subscribe; mobile apps use Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchase for in-app digital subscriptions where store rules require it, and all channels unlock the same entitlements everywhere. Nepal billing starts with prepaid periods through Khalti and eSewa. Details are in the [subscription model](../../01-company/subscription-model.md).

Consequences: the control plane needs a plan and entitlement catalogue, usage metering, app-store receipt verification, and a subscription ledger before paid plans launch. Alternatives considered: separate subscriptions per product app (rejected: fragmented billing and repeated charges for the same person) and plan-name checks inside products (rejected: every pricing change would need product releases).
