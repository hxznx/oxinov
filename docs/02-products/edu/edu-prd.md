# Oxinov Edu product requirements document

**Status:** Draft for product review. **Portfolio:** Oxinov Edu is the first product on the [Oxinov company platform](../../01-company/platform-blueprint.md). **Source:** [project brief](edu-brief.md) and [FRD](../../03-requirements/frd/edu-frd.md).

## Personas and jobs

| Persona | Primary job |
| --- | --- |
| Tenant owner | Create, brand, subscribe to, and govern an LMS. |
| Tenant administrator | Manage programs, instructors, students, exams, and finances. |
| Instructor | Publish recorded courses, assignments, and practice tests; support learners. |
| Learner | Find a course, purchase access, study, chat, submit work, and see results. |
| Platform operator | Operate tenants, plans, releases, and support controls. |

## Product capabilities

1. Self-service tenant creation from a verified email account, membership-based workspace selection, and safe branding customization.
2. Courses for JLPT N5–N1, current SSW fields, named languages, and configurable IT pathways.
3. Recorded video/audio, multilingual lessons, chapter practice, timed mock exams, assignments, results, and certificates.
4. Student chat, support, analytics, administration, tenant billing, course payments, and instructor payouts.
5. Web plus Android/iOS parity, with Google Play release readiness.
6. AI prompts that draft configuration and content for human review, within tenant permissions.

## Scope and release sequencing

All numbered FRD requirements are target scope. The [roadmap](edu-roadmap.md) proposes staged delivery; it does not remove target scope. Live classes and offline downloads are not yet specified. Actual course material and official-exam licensing require separate content work.
