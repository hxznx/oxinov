# Oxinov Platform Portal (`frontend/platform-web`)

Single-page customer portal, unified product launcher, and account management web app served at `app.oxinov.com` and `id.oxinov.com`.

## Scope
- Passwordless sign-in (Google OAuth, Email OTP, Apple sign-in).
- Account security, session management, and notification preferences.
- Progressive trust level verification (T1 Member -> T2 Contact -> T3 Personal KYC -> T4 Business KYC).
- Universal product launcher and organization switcher.
- Subscription billing center (Oxinov One + product plans), invoice history, and Nepal payment (Khalti/eSewa) top-ups.
- Privacy controls, data export, and account deletion requests.
