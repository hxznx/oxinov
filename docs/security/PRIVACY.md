# Privacy

Tenant owners control their educational data subject to platform operations and law. Collect only necessary account, learning, chat, payment, and support data. Define lawful basis, age policy, regions, retention periods, export/deletion process, processor list, and cross-border transfer rules before launch.

AI prompts must not mix tenants or expose private learner/payment data without an approved purpose. Public certificate verification reveals only approved fields. See [retention](../data/DATA-RETENTION.md).
