# ADR-011: One Oxinov account with simple sign-in and progressive trust

**Date:** 2026-09-24. **Status:** Accepted. Customers sign in at `id.oxinov.com` with Continue with Google or an email one-time code; Continue with Apple is added before an iOS app ships; customer passwords are not offered. One Oxinov account works across every launched product through OIDC single sign-on, and first sign-in grants a free member entitlement to all launched products. Sign-in stays low-friction by deferring checks: a verified phone (T2), individual KYC (T3), or business KYC (T4), plus just-in-time acceptance of a product-role policy, are required only for actions that affect other people or move money. The platform control plane owns trust levels, policy acceptances, and KYC; products enforce the required level per action. Staff use separate identities with mandatory MFA. Details are in [identity and access](../identity-and-access.md) and [platform policies](../../01-company/platform-policies.md).

Consequences: the platform needs SMS verification, KYC review, and policy-version storage before any marketplace launches; Keycloak must be configured with Google brokering, verified-email-only linking, and an email one-time-code flow (an extension, if required, needs review under the dependency policy). This refines ADR-008: "explicit entitlements" remain, but the member entitlement is granted automatically. Alternatives considered: per-product sign-up (rejected: repeated friction and duplicated accounts), passwords plus email verification (rejected: reset load and credential-stuffing risk), and full KYC at sign-up (rejected: blocks casual users who only want to learn or browse).
