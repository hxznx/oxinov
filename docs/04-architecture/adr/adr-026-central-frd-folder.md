# ADR-026: Central FRD folder and five proposed module definitions

**Date:** 2026-09-28. **Status:** Accepted for requirements discovery only (owner request to create separate FRDs for Oxinov Market, Oxinov Services Market, Oxinov Studio, Oxinov JP, and Oxinov Tech). This decision does not close a product release gate, approve spending, or authorize application scaffolding.

1. **Every FRD has one home.** Functional requirements live under `docs/requirements/frd/`; the requirements standard and shared NFR remain directly under `docs/requirements/`. Existing Platform, Edu, and HR FRDs move without changing their IDs or behavior.
2. **Market and Services Market gain proposed FRDs.** Oxinov Market is the shorter customer-facing label in its FRD for the chartered Oxinov Commodity Market at `market.oxinov.com`; the `market` technical slug and Commodity Market charter remain authoritative until brand approval. Services Market keeps the `services` slug and planned address. Both release gates remain closed.
3. **Studio is a production workspace, not a broadcaster.** The proposed Studio scope covers briefs, estimates, projects, secure assets, reviews, rights, client approvals, delivery, billing, and retention. Streaming or broadcasting needs a separate decision and regulatory approval.
4. **JP is a cross-product journey portal.** Until the owner refines the definition, Oxinov JP means primary-source Japan guidance, private readiness checklists, consent-based references to Edu and HR records, reviewed partners, appointments, and referrals. It does not decide visas, provide unlicensed immigration or recruitment services, or duplicate Edu and HR systems of record.
5. **Tech is the client delivery portal.** Until the owner refines the definition, Oxinov Tech covers Oxinov engineering and technology-service intake, proposals, project delivery, secure collaboration, support, reporting, invoicing, and closure. Hosting, managed security operations, remote administration, and regulated consulting remain separate gated capabilities.
6. **Permanent requirement blocks are reserved.** Market uses `FR-MKT-5000–5999`, Services Market uses `FR-SVC-7000–7999`, Studio uses `FR-STUDIO-8500–8599`, JP uses `FR-JP-8600–8699`, and Tech uses `FR-TECH-8700–8799`. The remaining `8800–8999` range stays reserved.

Consequences: product discovery now has testable allowed and denied behavior before code exists, while explicit disclaimers and open-decision sections prevent a draft from masquerading as approval. Moving existing FRDs changes documentation paths only and requires repository-wide link and validator updates. Alternatives considered: leaving FRDs across the requirements root (rejected by the owner's organization request), treating every named module as approved (unsafe because owners, evidence, legal review, operations, cost, and product definitions are incomplete), and making JP or Tech copy Edu/HR/platform data (rejected because product boundaries remain authoritative).
