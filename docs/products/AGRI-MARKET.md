# Oxinov Agri Market charter (Superseded)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Scope Update:** This charter has been formally expanded and superseded by the **[Oxinov Commodity Market Charter](COMMODITY-MARKET.md)** at `market.oxinov.com`.
> Per corporate platform directives, the marketplace scope was extended beyond agricultural produce to encompass **all physical commodities, industrial raw materials, construction aggregates, commercial machinery, and circular second-hand / pre-owned equipment**.

## Historical Context

**Origin:** Flo Softwares `comodity-market` (KrishiConnect) and `BT-Bazz-ComodityMarket-server`.

Originally drafted as a specialized agricultural exchange connecting Nepali farmers and cooperatives with produce buyers, the product model was redesigned into the **Oxinov Commodity Market** to address the broader national need for:
1. Primary agricultural produce and cash crops (tea, coffee, cardamom, grains, spices).
2. Raw materials and construction commodities (timber, scrap metal, stone, aggregates).
3. Commercial machinery and agricultural equipment (power tillers, tractors, pumps, processing units).
4. Circular re-commerce and second-hand equipment (pre-owned commercial vehicles, verified used farm machinery, surplus liquidation lots).
