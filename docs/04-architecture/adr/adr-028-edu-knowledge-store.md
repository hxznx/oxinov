# ADR-028: Oxinov Edu becomes Oxinov's own knowledge store

**Date:** 2026-10-01. **Status:** Accepted (owner decisions of 2026-10-01). Extends [ADR-023](adr-023-khalti-esewa.md) (Oxinov sells its own courses first) and implements FR-CATALOG-305 to FR-CATALOG-309, FR-COURSE-206 to FR-COURSE-208, FR-PLAYER-405, FR-COMM-703, FR-MGMT-1403, FR-MOBILE-1504, FR-AUTH-104, FR-CATALOG-310, FR-CATALOG-311, FR-PLAYER-406, FR-ANALYTICS-804, FR-ANALYTICS-805, FR-COMM-704, FR-MGMT-1404, and FR-AI-1705.

The owner's decisions, in their words where quoted:

- Edu is "my platform course selling, idea selling, skill selling, think tank selling, my intellectual property selling". `edu.oxinov.com` opens on Oxinov's own store, not on a list of school workspaces.
- Every offering is sold by access period: one month NPR 5,000, six months NPR 10,000, one year NPR 15,000, and lifetime NPR 20,000.
- Bank QR payments are approved by hand ("Manual approve"). International learners will pay by card ("Students abroad pay by card").
- Videos stay on Oxinov's YouTube channel and documents on Google Drive. Subscribers see every lesson and material; free visitors see only what the administrator marks free (introduction, syllabus, live class links, selected videos or documents). Learners can view material but not download it.
- The interface is "fusion of the futuristic, gaming, cyberpunk".

## Decision

1. **One seller, many offering kinds.** The Oxinov workspace listed in `payments.sellerTenantIds` (ADR-023) is the store. An offering is a course with a kind: `COURSE`, `TRAINING`, `IDEA` (an idea or business concept), `THINK_TANK` (research and analysis), or `SKILL` (short practical skill). All kinds share authoring, review, access, payment, and the player. Categories group them: Languages (Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Russian, Nepali, English, Hindi), Technology (programming, IoT, robotics, machine learning, animation, RC aircraft), and Ideas and research. School workspaces and join codes remain in the code and data model but leave the learner navigation until another school is accepted as a seller.
2. **Access plans per offering.** Each offering has up to four active plans (1, 6, or 12 months, or lifetime), each with its own NPR price. New offerings start with the owner's default prices, and the administrator can change prices per offering. A paid plan creates a PURCHASE entitlement whose `endsAt` is the plan end; lifetime leaves `endsAt` empty. Buying again while access is active extends from the current end date, not from today, so a renewal never loses paid days. The price and plan are copied onto the payment when it is created, so later price changes never alter what a learner agreed to pay.
3. **Manual bank QR payments with review.** The checkout shows Ox Inov Pvt. Ltd.'s bank QR image and account name, the exact amount, and a payment reference made from the order (for example `OXE-7F3K2Q`). The learner pays in their bank app and submits the bank transaction ID and a screenshot. The payment is `PENDING_REVIEW`; nothing is granted. An administrator compares it with the company bank statement and approves or rejects it with a reason. Approval grants the entitlement in the same transaction, once. A transaction ID can be used for one payment only. Rejection grants nothing and tells the learner why. Khalti and eSewa (ADR-023) stay available as automatic methods when merchant keys exist.
4. **Card payments come later, through a gateway.** International cards need a card-acquiring gateway account (for example the payment gateway of Oxinov's Nepali bank). Oxinov never sees or stores card numbers; the gateway's hosted page takes the card, and access follows only the server's own verification of the result (FR-PAY-2701). The adapter is built when the owner has that account (FR-CATALOG-308).
5. **Externally hosted material, revealed only to entitled learners.** A lesson or material can point to an **unlisted** YouTube video or a Google Drive file (PDF, slides, document, image), besides Oxinov's own storage and links. YouTube **private** videos cannot be used: YouTube limits them to 50 named Google accounts and blocks embedding them on other sites. The Edu API returns the YouTube video ID or Drive file ID only after the same entitlement check as Oxinov-hosted media, and only for items the learner may see; paid items never appear in public pages, page source, sitemaps, or previews. The administrator keeps Drive files at "anyone with the link can view" with "viewers cannot download, print, or copy" turned on.
6. **View-only display, honestly described.** Material opens inside Oxinov's player and viewer: YouTube through the privacy-enhanced `youtube-nocookie.com` embed with no link out, Drive through its preview frame, Oxinov files through short-lived signed URLs served inline. No download buttons are offered, and a watermark with the learner's email covers the viewer. These measures deter casual copying; they cannot stop screen recording or a determined learner who extracts a link, and the product says so to the owner. Material that needs stronger protection moves to Oxinov's own storage (CloudFront signed URLs and HLS when ADR-021's trigger is met).
7. **Free and subscriber visibility per item.** Every lesson, material, and live session is either `FREE` (visible to signed-in visitors and preview on the offering page) or `SUBSCRIBERS` (requires an active entitlement). The introduction and syllabus are always free.
8. **Live classes are links, not hosting.** An offering can list live sessions with a title, start time, duration, and a Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or Zoom link, each FREE or SUBSCRIBERS. Oxinov does not host video calls.
9. **Notifications through the platform.** After the first-sign-in welcome, the platform sends "Welcome to Oxinov" by email. After a payment is approved or verified, Edu sends "Thank you for subscribing to {offering} ({plan})" with the access end date and the support address, plus an in-app notice; a rejection sends the reason. Seven days and one day before a time-limited plan ends, Edu sends a renewal reminder. Emails go through the existing `mail-relay`.
10. **Mobile first as an installable web app.** `edu.oxinov.com` becomes an installable web app (manifest, icons, offline fallback page) that works at phone width, so learners can add it to their home screen now. The native Expo app (ADR-003) follows using the same API. Purchases happen on the web: Google Play requires its own billing for digital content bought inside an app, so the app links to web checkout only where store rules allow and otherwise shows existing access (FR-MOBILE-1503).
11. **Fusion cyberpunk interface.** The store uses the shared design system at **Full** intensity for the store hero and **Standard** for other discovery pages (HUD frames, neon accents, grid, game-style progress and level badges), and **Calm** intensity for checkout, payment review, reading, and exams, as the [design system](../../07-design/design-system.md) already requires.

12. **Account, community, and admin tools (owner, same day).** Learners get an account centre in the Windows-Settings style (subscriptions with plan and end date, payments, activity, notifications, messages, certificates, invite friends, sign out, sign out everywhere, data export, account deletion), free classes, ideas, and skills, share and recommend links, levels and points with no money value, admin notices, chat with support and instructors, and **OXI**, a course advisor that is rule-based until the AI gateway exists (ADR-014). Administrators get Oxinov Studio in the same style: a dashboard with a "Needs you" list, offerings with expandable rows, dropdowns, and switches, notices, free access grants, the payment queue, an inbox, OXI settings, referrals, and roles.

## Consequences

- Approving payments by hand costs the owner time and delays access by minutes to hours; the review queue shows the oldest first and the learner sees the state at all times. Automatic gateways remove this delay when merchant accounts exist.
- Unlisted YouTube and shared Drive links can be passed on by a paying learner. This is accepted for cost: YouTube and Drive cost nothing, while Oxinov-hosted video costs about US$0.11 per GB watched.
- The data model gains offering kinds, categories, access plans, plan snapshots on payments, a `PENDING_REVIEW` payment state with review fields, external media sources, item visibility, and live sessions. Existing courses become `COURSE` offerings with no plans until the administrator adds them; existing free courses keep working.
- Alternatives considered: YouTube private videos shared per learner (50-account limit, no embedding, manual), Google Drive sharing per learner email (needs a Google account per learner and Drive API automation under admin@oxinov.com; kept as a later option for high-value material), and a separate store product (rejected: Edu already has courses, player, exams, and certificates).
